The short version: we collect what you choose to send us so we can reply to you. We don't sell your information. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing Aamir@precisiondigitalm.com.

01What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form, we receive your name, business name, email address, phone number (if you add it), industry, approximate ad budget, and your message. When you call, text, or email us, we receive whatever you include.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our pages and the services they load receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you came from, and the pages you view.

02How we use it

  • To reply to your message and prepare for a strategy call
  • To schedule and confirm calls you book
  • To provide services if you become a client
  • To understand which pages and ads bring people to our site, so we can improve them
  • To keep the site secure and working

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your inquiry. We won't add you to a marketing list without your permission.

03Services we rely on

We use a small number of outside services to run this site. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Calendly handles booking when you schedule a call.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and a graphics library used on some pages.
  • Google provides advertising and measurement tools, described below.

04Cookies and ad tracking

We may advertise on Google and use Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Analytics to measure how people find us and whether they contact us. These tools can use cookies or similar technology to record that a visit came from an ad and whether a form was submitted or a call was booked.

You can limit this by adjusting your browser's cookie settings, using Google's ad settings, or installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

05Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above so they can do their job for us, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety. If the business were ever sold or merged, information could transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

06How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as they're useful for following up, and client records for as long as we work together plus any period required for tax or legal purposes. After that, we delete them.

07Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email Aamir@precisiondigitalm.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can reply STOP to any text message from us.

08Security

This site runs over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and we limit who can access your information. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as passwords or payment details through our contact form.

09Children

This website is for business owners and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

10Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.
