01Anytime Fitness · W.A.G. BrandRolling Meadows, IL
6-week challengeMeta AdsSmart booking agentPay per walk-in

From $12.5K to $34K.

The ask

The Rolling Meadows club's personal training program was steady at about $12K to $13K in recurring monthly revenue. They wanted a six-week challenge to push it higher.

What we built

Meta campaigns feeding an automated smart booking agent that scheduled every interested lead straight onto the floor. The challenge was paid in three installments during the six weeks.

How we got paid

Not per form fill. Per verified walk-in. A simple geo check confirmed each lead physically arrived, so the club only paid for people who showed up. Closing was in the fitness manager's hands.

The result

Recurring training revenue climbed to $34K during the 1.5 months the campaign ran. More than 12% of challenge clients then signed 12 to 18 month programs worth $3,500 to $4,500 each.

02Opus OneTurks & Caicos
Short-term · 2 phasesCustom ordering systemB2B partnerships

34 clients in 14 days.

The problem

A premium liquor store with a great product and one hard problem: visitors on the island weren't buying. Tourists didn't know the store, and getting there wasn't part of their trip.

Phase 1

We built a custom delivery-style ordering and booking system for his brand, so guests could order without ever finding the storefront. It brought in 34 new clients in the first 14 days. That's all he needed, so that's where we stopped.

Phase 2

Two months later he came back for something new: B2B. We made Opus One a preferred vendor at two local villas, with QR codes and in-villa flyers that put his catalog in front of every arriving guest.

The point

We don't need to work together for life. Bring us the problem in front of you, we fix it, and we're here when the next one shows up.

04Core and Flow by Marbel RamosSpain
3 monthsCustom mobile appPaid ads

A studio that fits in a phone.

The goal

After COVID, Marbel wanted to teach yoga remotely and needed a way to showcase and sell her classes without a physical studio.

What we built

A custom mobile app for her classes and programs, included in the engagement. She didn't need a website. The app did the job.

The spend

$875 a month for three months, $2,625 in total, to fill three tiers priced at $130 to $150 per month.

The result

18 new clients from the campaign across beginner, intermediate, and advanced teacher pathway. She finished with 26 total clients. Each new client paid back their acquisition cost in about one month of membership.
