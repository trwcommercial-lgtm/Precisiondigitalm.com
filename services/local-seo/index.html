Why the map matters

The first three
get the call.

When someone nearby searches for what you sell, Google shows a map with three businesses before anything else. Most people pick one of those three and never scroll any further.

If you're not in that box, you're invisible to the customers closest to your door, no matter how good your work is.

How the map decides

What puts a business
in the top 3.

Google ranks local results on three things. Our 90-day plan works on all of them.

Relevance

Do you match the search?

Your Google Business Profile categories, services, and description, plus your website, need to clearly say you do what the searcher wants.

Distance

Are you close by?

Google favors businesses near the person searching. You can't move your address, but accurate, consistent listings help Google trust where you are.

Prominence

Are you trusted?

Reviews, ratings, mentions across the web, and an active profile tell Google your business is established and worth showing.

Straight answers

Local SEO
questions.

How much does local SEO cost?
A small setup fee covers the groundwork, and the success fee is only due if your business reaches the top 3 on Google Maps for your target keyword within 90 days. Your exact numbers are quoted on a free call before you commit.
How long does local SEO take?
Profile fixes often show some movement within the first few weeks, but it depends on your market and competition. Our offer is built around a 90-day window, and we check where you stand before we start.
Who picks the keyword?
We do. We research what customers in your area type when they are ready to buy, then pick the keyword with the best mix of buyer intent and winnability. It is written into your agreement before work starts.
What is the difference between local SEO and Google Ads?
Google Ads puts you at the top of search results for as long as you pay for clicks. Local SEO earns you a spot in the Google Maps results, where clicks are free. Many businesses use ads for fast leads while local SEO builds up.
Do you work outside Schaumburg?
Yes. We are based in Schaumburg and have done client work in Rolling Meadows, Elk Grove Village, Oak Brook, and Darien. Local SEO works for any business with a real address that serves nearby customers.
Can anyone guarantee a #1 ranking?
No. Nobody controls Google, so a guaranteed ranking is a guess. We tie our pay to something we can prove instead: the success fee is only due once a ranking check shows you in the top 3.
