Now

Precision Digital Marketing

Every lesson in one system: ads, websites, and automation built to turn attention into paying customers.

Precision Digital Marketing was built for local businesses in the Chicago suburbs. Not national brands. Not e-commerce. Local businesses that serve real communities and need marketing that understands their market.

After watching too many good local businesses get burned by generalist agencies applying national strategies to hyper-local problems, Aamir built PDM around a different model: deep focus on local markets, direct access to the person running your account, and a results-first approach with no long-term contracts.

"If I can't see a clear path to growing your business, I'll tell you on the first call."

That same playbook has been tested well beyond Illinois, from a luxury liquor brand in Turks and Caicos to a yoga studio in Spain. But home base is, and always will be, the suburbs around Schaumburg, with client work in Rolling Meadows, Elk Grove Village, Oak Brook, and Darien.

Whether you need one problem fixed in six weeks or a full system built for the long haul, you work with Aamir directly from the first call to the last report.
