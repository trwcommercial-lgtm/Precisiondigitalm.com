    Straight answers / 007

    Questions
    answered.

    How fast can a campaign start producing results?
    Well-built campaigns can start generating inquiries within days of launch. One of our Jamaica hospitality campaigns produced 51 inquiries in under 24 hours. Steady, profitable customer acquisition usually takes a few weeks of testing and optimization.
    Do you only work with businesses in Schaumburg?
    No. We are based in Schaumburg, Illinois and serve businesses across the Chicago suburbs, plus hospitality brands well beyond, including clients in Turks and Caicos and Jamaica.
    What if my ads are already running?
    Then we start by auditing the full path: targeting, offer, landing page, response time, and follow-up. Most wasted budget leaks out after the click, so fixing the conversion path often improves results without raising spend.
    Can you build a booking system that takes deposits?
    Yes. We build booking pages that handle inquiries, collect deposits, apply your current promotion, and place confirmed bookings on your calendar, with automated reminders.
    What happens on the free strategy call?
    We look at your current marketing, find where budget or leads are leaking, and outline the changes we would make first. You leave with a clear plan whether or not you hire us.
