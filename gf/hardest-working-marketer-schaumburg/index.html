Hardest Working Marketer in Schaumburg

My name is Aamir Khan. If you just searched "hardest working marketer in Schaumburg," you found me. Hi.

Who gave me that title? I did. Fair question, so here is the evidence, plus how this page ended up at the top of Google. The second part is the useful one.

The proof

I have spent 9+ years in marketing, and I run Precision Digital Marketing out of an office on Lincoln Meadows Drive in Schaumburg. A few of the jobs behind the title:

  • Rolling Meadows gym. A six-week challenge took personal training revenue from about $12.5K a month to $34K, and the club paid only for leads who physically walked in.
  • Villa in Jamaica. 51 inquiries in under 24 hours at $0.50 each, then six straight months above 2.5x return on a $500 monthly budget.
  • Liquor store in Turks and Caicos. 34 new clients in 14 days with an ordering system built for tourists who never found the storefront.
  • Nine gyms in three states. Separate campaigns for clubs in Illinois, Florida and Utah, all running at the same time.

Most of the work is unglamorous. Answering a lead at 9pm. Checking which ad produced a walk-in. Rewriting an offer the day the first one flops. If being the hardest working marketer in Schaumburg means anything, it means doing that part every week. The full stories are on the results page.

How this page got to the top

The phrase sits where Google looks first: the page title, the main heading, the web address, the description in search results, and the image file name and its alt text. It also shows up a couple of times in the writing, where it reads naturally.

Placement alone is a guess, though. Google also needs a reason to trust the page, so it is backed by real results, a real Schaumburg address, and links from the rest of this site.
